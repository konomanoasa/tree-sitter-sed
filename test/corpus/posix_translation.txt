:language(sed_posix)
==================

---

(script
  (command_list
    (command
      body: (translate_command
        name: (command_name)
        argument: (translate_argument
          opening_delimiter: (delimiter)
          source: (translate_source
            (translate_literal))
          middle_delimiter: (delimiter)
          destination: (translate_destination
            (translate_literal))
          closing_delimiter: (delimiter))))
    (separator)))
